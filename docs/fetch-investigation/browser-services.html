Who does the work?

Supported DIY keeps you involved. Staff-operated equipment and specialist fabrication or ECU work have their own agreed scope. Confirm the responsibilities for your task before booking. Technical consultation is paid.

SUPPORTED DIY

Maintenance & parts fitting

Work on the motorcycle you own in Japan, with equipment and practical guidance alongside you. Agree on the task, then work through the parts you can do with support where needed.

  • Oil changes and chain cleaning or adjustment
  • Battery checks and brake maintenance support
  • Parts fitting, electrical accessories and wiring

Single sessions, passes and memberships cover selected tasks. Parts and consumables may cost extra.

ASSESS THE NEXT STEP

Falls, damage & things that feel wrong

A fall, a damaged part or a handling concern can leave you unsure where to start. Arrange a visit to check the actual motorcycle and discuss repair, replacement or machining options.

  • Explain what happened and the symptoms
  • Agree on an assessment and the support available
  • Confirm the work and costs before proceeding

Technical assessment and advice are paid services. Feasibility depends on the motorcycle and the issue.

STAFF-OPERATED EQUIPMENT

Tyres & workshop equipment

Use the workshop as part of supported work, with the right preparation for your motorcycle. Staff operate the tyre changer; the arrangements for other tasks are agreed before work begins.

  • Tyre changes and tyre-changer use
  • Check wheel, tyre and equipment compatibility
  • Confirm any parts, materials or preparation time

Selected tasks are available to single-session, pass and member users. Ask about separate parts or specialist charges.

SPECIALIST PROJECTS

Custom part design & fabrication

Bring a part idea or a problem that an off-the-shelf component does not solve. Discuss design, fabrication, welding and one-off machining with eFactory.

  • Share the purpose, motorcycle and requirements
  • Discuss feasibility, materials and fitting
  • Agree on a project scope and individual quotation

Specialist work and materials may be quoted separately. Enquiring does not confirm that a project can be accepted.

SPECIFICATION & PURPOSE

ECU Flashing & setup

Explore ECU Flashing services that take the motorcycle’s specification and intended use into account. eFactory’s published approach emphasises rideability, particularly in motogymkhana.

  • Model, year, ECU and modification checks
  • Discuss the riding characteristics you want to improve
  • Agree on compatibility, scope and pricing

Availability and additional charges are confirmed individually. No universal model coverage or performance result is promised.

MEMBER SUPPORT

Gymkhana, riding & bike setup

Keep working on the relationship between you and your motorcycle. Riding and gymkhana advice, riding-position adjustments and suspension-setting enquiries are listed as member services.

  • Talk through riding and setup concerns
  • Discuss a gymkhana or track-use project
  • Confirm the equipment and support available for your goal

Membership, scope and any specialist charges need to be confirmed. Specific track services are agreed individually.
