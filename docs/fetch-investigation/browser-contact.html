HELP US UNDERSTAND YOUR VISIT

What to send

The initial enquiry checks whether the available service fits your request. Technical diagnosis and detailed advice are paid services.

  • Your name
  • Motorcycle make, model and relevant specifications
  • The task, issue or project you have in mind
  • Your preferred date
  • Any concerns, preparation questions or language requirements

Before you make the journey

Agree on an appointment

All visits are by appointment. Sending a message does not secure a slot. Wait for the date and arrangements to be agreed before travelling.

Confirm the project & costs

Check the service scope, plan eligibility, equipment and any parts or preparation needed. Ask what extra costs apply to the work.

Check language arrangements

The scope of English enquiries, in-person explanations and specialist technical consultation needs to be confirmed. An English website does not guarantee full English technical support.

Plan from wherever you live

Riders from across Japan are welcome at the Suzuka workshop. Confirm the arrangements before booking travel or accommodation.
