01 / YOUR RIDING

What would you like to change?

  • Jerky response as you first open the throttle
  • A motorcycle that feels difficult to manage at low speed
  • Engine braking that feels too strong
  • Checking fuelling after modifications
  • Adjusting the response for motogymkhana

The cause may lie outside the ECU. Vehicle condition is checked before considering what flashing can address.

02 / OUR APPROACH

Ride it. Feel it. Refine it.

Changing ECU data is one step. What matters is how that change connects with the rider’s inputs.

Share your goals

Describe when and how the motorcycle feels different from what you want—for example, initial throttle response or drive at low speed.

Check what can be adjusted

Fuel, ignition, engine braking and other controls vary by motorcycle and ECU. Adjustments are considered against the actual specification.

Ride and verify

Where needed, measurements and logging support riding checks and rider feedback as the response is refined.

What is “transient response”?

It is the engine’s response when your inputs change, such as opening or closing the throttle, rather than only at a steady engine speed. The feeling from input to drive matters alongside maximum power. Measurement and riding-check methods are agreed for the motorcycle and the work.

05 / QUESTIONS

Before you arrange ECU work.

Which motorcycles are supported?

Compatibility and adjustable parameters depend on the model, year, ECU and vehicle specification. The examples do not establish support for every model or year. Send your model, year and modified parts first.

Can I enquire after changing the exhaust?

Yes, you can enquire about your current specification. Include the exhaust manufacturer and product, plus intake or engine changes. Flashing compatibility and measurement methods are checked individually.

Can the setup be adjusted again afterwards?

A GROM re-setting example is publicly documented. Confirm availability for your own motorcycle, costs, number of adjustments and the applicable time window before work begins.

Can I send only the ECU?

The official X account has announced ECU sales for a specific GROM JC61 setup. Selling an ECU and flashing your own ECU by post are separate services. Postal flashing availability has not been verified: contact eFactory before sending anything.

Points to confirm before work

Confirm how the original ECU will be handled, the price, and implications for Japanese vehicle inspection, manufacturer warranty and fault risks. ECU Flashing does not guarantee performance, safety or legal compliance.
